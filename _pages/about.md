I am a Master's student in Cyberspace Security at [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://en.sjtu.edu.cn/) (SJTU). My research interests lie in **Agentic Systems & LLM Reasoning**, with a focus on tool-augmented LLMs, reinforcement learning for mathematical reasoning, and self-evolving memory mechanisms. Previously, I received my B.E. degree from [Xidian University](https://en.xidian.edu.cn/), where I worked closely with [Prof. Xiangyu Wang](https://web.xidian.edu.cn/wangxiangyu/).
